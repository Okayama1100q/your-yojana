YOUR-YOJANA
YOUR-YOJANA is an AI-Powered Multi-Agent Civic Welfare & Governance Ecosystem.
It provides citizens with one platform to:
discover potentially relevant welfare schemes
understand scheme requirements
receive eligibility guidance
manage required documents
receive application guidance
track applications
track benefits
receive renewal guidance
access civic services
report civic issues
connect with government, NGOs and partners

It focuses on:
Equity
Inclusion
Livelihood Support
Governance
Transparency
Civic Collaboration
Partnerships

It aligns with:
SDG 1 — No Poverty
SDG 5 — Gender Equality
SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities
SDG 16 — Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions
SDG 17 — Partnerships for the Goals

SIX AI AGENTS
Swasthika — Eligibility Agent
Identifies citizen needs and matches citizen profiles with potentially relevant government schemes.
It may consider:
income
employment
family information
certificates
socio-economic details
existing benefits
eligibility factors
It supports scheme matching.
It does NOT make final government eligibility decisions.

Saarthi — Guidance Agent
Guides citizens through:
document requirements
applications
verification
approvals
benefit delivery
application tracking
benefit tracking
renewals
reminders

Nyaya — Civic & Governance Agent
Supports:
civic complaint reporting
complaint classification
issue routing
department identification
issue tracking
transparency
accountability
Nyaya is not itself a government authority.

Drishti — Intelligence, Impact & Risk Agent
Supports:
beneficiary analysis
utilization analysis
impact measurement
anomaly detection
duplicate detection
unusual-activity identification
cases requiring human review
Do not automatically label detected anomalies as fraud.

Setu — Collaboration Agent
Connects citizens with:
government
NGOs
private organizations
CSR organizations
training organizations
livelihood partners
support organizations

Darshan — Transparency Agent
Provides aggregated insights into:
beneficiaries
budgets
utilization
outcomes
pending cases
civic resolution
collaboration
impact metrics
Never reveal private citizen information.
