How It Works

From report to resolution

Your Yojana makes civic complaint resolution simple, transparent, and accountable.

1
Citizen Reports

You describe the civic problem in plain language — no forms, no jargon. Just tell us what happened.

2
AI Understands

Our system analyzes the complaint — identifying the category, health risk, affected population, and duration.

3
Smart Routing

The complaint is directed to the appropriate government department based on category and location.

4
Government Resolves

Officers review the complaint, take action, and update the status. You can track every step of the way.
