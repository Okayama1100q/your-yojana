YOUR YOJANA — SYNTHETIC CHENNAI CIVIC COMPLAINT DATA

This dataset is synthetic demonstration data.
It is NOT a record of real Chennai complaints and must not be presented as real incidents.

Purpose:
- SafeRoute development/testing
- Civic Map visualization
- Route-to-complaint proximity experiments
- Priority/filter UI testing
- Demo/hackathon data

Important:
- Coordinates are synthetic and approximate.
- Categories, priorities, statuses, counts and durations are generated.
- Do not use this dataset for real-world safety decisions.
- When real civic portal data becomes available, replace the synthetic layer.

Suggested next step:
Use actual road geometry from OpenStreetMap for routing, then calculate which synthetic complaints fall within a chosen distance of each route.
