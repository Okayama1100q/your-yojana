What this uses

Travel times and route geometry come from OpenStreetMap routing. Civic indicators use the synthetic Chennai demonstration dataset (category, priority, status, coordinates) — generated records, not real government complaints. Your live complaint database is never modified or scored here. Citizen identity is never used. Crime data and IoT sensors are not part of this system.
