AI Governance & Decision Transparency
How Your Yojana's AI agents assess, prioritize, and route civic complaints
⚙ AI-assisted — not autonomous
CURRENTLY ACTIVE
Complaint Understanding Agent ACTIVE

Each complaint is analyzed by an LLM (Groq — openai/gpt-oss-120b). The agent extracts:

  • Category & core issue
  • Health risk / safety risk flags
  • Essential service disruption flag
  • Vulnerable population affected
  • Number and unit of people affected
  • Duration of issue (days)
  • Location (if explicitly mentioned)

The agent is strictly instructed not to invent information. If unknown, values default to 0 or false.

Priority Agent ACTIVE

A rule-based scoring engine produces a 0–100 priority score using transparent, auditable weights:

FactorPoints
Public safety risk+30
Health risk+25
Essential service disruption+20
≥1000 people/families affected+15
≥500 people/families affected+12
≥100 people/families affected+8
Any people/families affected+3
Vulnerable population+10
Duration (2 pts/day, max 10)up to +10

Score capped at 100. No factor is hidden or unexplainable.

Priority Levels & Response Times

Scores are mapped to priority levels with mandated response times:

CRITICAL · Score ≥ 80 Response within 24h
HIGH · Score 60–79 Response within 48h
MEDIUM · Score 40–59 Response within 72h
LOW · Score < 40 Response within 5 days
Routing Agent ACTIVE

A rule-based keyword router matches the complaint category and issue to the correct government department. Departments include:

• Water Supply Department • Sanitation Department • Roads & Highways Dept. • Electrical Department • Traffic Department • Public Health Department • Education Department • Social Welfare Department • Law Enforcement Dept. • Municipal Grievance Cell

Confidence: 95% for keyword matches; 50% for fallback to Municipal Grievance Cell.

Vision Agent ACTIVE

Analyses citizen photo evidence via Groq multimodal model qwen/qwen3.6-27b. Detects potholes, road damage, standing water, garbage, blocked drains, broken streetlights, and related hazards. Findings enrich Priority Agent scoring. Advisory only.

Human Review & Officer Override

AI assessments are advisory, not final. Government officers review every complaint in the Resolution Portal and make the final call on assignment and resolution. The AI recommendation is clearly labelled as AI-assisted on every case.

Status Workflow
Every complaint follows a transparent, human-controlled lifecycle:

PENDING → ASSIGNED → IN_PROGRESS → RESOLVED

Officers update status through the Resolution Portal. Each transition is confirmed and logged.
